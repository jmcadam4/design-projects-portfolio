#About Me
I am in between my sophomore and junior year by credit hours as of fall 2026 and have enjoyed the pursuit of understanding mechanical systems and their designs for many years before I came to UNCC. I worked on bikes and cars a lot in middle school then found my way to robotics in high school. I knew for a long time that I wanted to be a mechanical engineer however the experience of working with a team on a tight timeline to get a working robot built and deployed really solidified that belief. I find a lot of satisfaction and purpose in solving problems, being able to identify the best means of solving it through research and testing. Organizing projects and ensuring things are running in unison as smoothly as possible while blockers for one division aren't preventing progress for another. More than learning how to solve problems, learning how to do so efficiently has become the most valuable skillset I've developed over the past half a decade. Being able to make a working solution is relatively easy, being able to do it in an economical way quickly is the hard part. More hours need to be put into management and planning a project than those that go into the actual design and manufacturing in order to be successful. Although I am just beginning my professional career and therefor am not typically the one setting timelines and managing dependencies, being able to understand the conflicts of my portion and other aspects of the project has helped me work better alongside my peers in a meaningful way.
