# James McAdam – Engineering Design Portfolio

![MEGR 2156-7 Title](MEGR2156title.png)

This site is the record of my work in sophomore design at UNC Charlotte. Each assignment is documented under the same three headings — **Analyze**, **Decide**, **Communicate** — so a reader can follow not just what I built, but how I got there and why I chose what I chose.

> **Engineering is the art of making decisions you can defend.**

## Why this course, and where it sits

Sophomore design is where the coursework stops being isolated problem sets and starts being design work. Statics, mechanics of materials and materials science each give you a way to check whether something works; this course is about deciding what to build in the first place, and being able to justify it once it is built. That is the skill I am here to practice, and this portfolio is the evidence of it.

It matters to me because the part of engineering I actually enjoy is the deciding — weighing options against real constraints like cost, manufacturability and service life, not just solving for a number. Every assignment here is a chance to get better at making that reasoning visible to somebody else, which is most of what professional engineering documentation is.

## My documentation standard

Every assignment on this site gets a thorough write-up: what I did, the reasoning at each step, and the problems I ran into along the way — not just the final answer.
